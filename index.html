For Fareeha

Someone has something to tell you 💌

💧

Dear Fareeha,

I’m sorry for the 33 days I stayed silent. I should have talked to you instead of disappearing, and I’m sorry for the worry and distance it caused.

Thank you for letting me back in. You asked about my meals, scolded me for sleeping late, listened to my days, and told me to step out of my comfort zone. You helped me become a better person, and I’m grateful for that every day.

When you’re not around, I feel so lonely. Hearing about your day is one of my favorite parts of mine.

I just want you to be happy and healthy, and I’ll be right here, taking care of you the way you’ve always taken care of me.

I love you. 💛

Forgive me?
